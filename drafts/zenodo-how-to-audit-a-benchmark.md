## Description

Preprint. Ten benchmarks audited in eleven audit passes: SWE-bench Verified (as a runner), DeepSWE and its revision, SWE-bench Pro, ProgramBench, τ-bench's contamination story, Terminal-Bench, MirrorCode, FrontierCode, Frontier-Bench, and SlopCodeBench. They broke in different places, and DeepSWE, re-audited after its revision, mostly held. This paper compresses the lenses into a checklist a stranger can run. A benchmark is a measurement instrument, and an instrument makes a contract of seven clauses: claim, spec, oracle, frame, gold, score, decay. Each clause can be broken, each break is a distinct sin, and each sin has a check. The eleven checks are ordered by cost, and the order carries the lesson: every severe defect in these audits surfaced before any model ran. The checklist ends by reconciling each clause against the published metaevaluation registries (BenchRisk, BetterBench) and filing the unregistered failure modes upstream.
