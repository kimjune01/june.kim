# Vector Space — angel outreach

*Working list, researched 2026-07-13. Publicly listed addresses only. An application form is not silently replaced with a guessed email.*

## What this outreach is for

The immediate ask is not simply money. It is an investor who can help assemble the next two ingredients:

1. a credible privacy/ad-policy reviewer — Ashkan Soltani or an equivalent person with enough standing that a publisher's counsel will listen; and
2. one publisher willing to run a small, disclosed pilot on real conversational traffic.

Vector Space separates the publisher's answer from a sponsored recommendation, keeps intent local until the user consents, and produces an auditable record of the recommendation and outcome. The first test can be manual. The hypothesis is falsified if a publisher will not run it, users punish it, or the advertiser will not renew.

The evidence is not yet “the market wants this.” What exists is:

- a body of PPC practitioners describing broken joins, unverifiable platform numbers, CRM reconciliation, and 30–60-day guess-and-check loops;
- a concrete protocol and working demonstration;
- a first-pilot offer of up to $10,000 to a publisher; and
- a sharp bottleneck: no publisher has agreed to put real traffic through it.

That last sentence belongs in the email. An investor who only supplies capital does not remove it.

## The actual ask in a meeting

Do not end with “any advice?” Ask for one of these in order:

1. *Would you introduce me to a privacy or ad-policy expert who can issue a public, scoped review of this architecture?*
2. *If the review is favorable, which publisher in your network could authorize one disclosed, manually settled pilot?*
3. *Would you fund the review and pilot as a milestone, rather than fund a platform before the market test?*

The reviewer does not have to be Ashkan Soltani. Equivalent means independent, technically capable, conversant in advertising/privacy, and credible to publisher counsel. Ashkan has already received three unanswered approaches; do not send a fourth cold email. A warm introduction or a materially new artifact is the only sensible next contact.

## What to send when someone replies

Send a two-page pilot memo, not the full archive:

- one diagram: answer → consent → sponsored recommendation → receipt;
- what data remains local and what crosses the boundary;
- the publisher's integration burden;
- the exact $10,000 pilot terms;
- user disclosure and opt-out language;
- what the receipt proves and explicitly does not prove;
- success and kill criteria; and
- links to the [Vector Space overview](https://june.kim/vector-space), [Stone Soup](https://june.kim/stone-soup), and the working demonstration.

Do not lead with HIPAA accusations or the earlier publisher's alleged data sale. That claim may matter to a reviewer, but it is not needed to explain the product and is difficult to prove from the outside. Lead with the visible, testable architecture.
