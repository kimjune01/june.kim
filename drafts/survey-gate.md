title: "The Survey Gate"
tags: methodology, crafting
---

Cast when the related-work section feels finished. That feeling comes from nearest-neighbor coverage and says nothing about the parent literature.

Take every noun phrase in the title and contribution statements. Move each one and two abstraction levels up. Search each level with `survey` appended.

Ladder backward too: search each mechanism under its pre-2015 and pre-1995 names. Vocabulary drift hides what abstraction can't reach.

Keep a hit only if it would change a sentence. The class that always does: the survey that makes your result expected, contradicted, or routine. Everything else is an insurance cite; drop it.

The search licenses one claim: no hit in this searched space. Never convert it to: no prior art exists.

Insert at zero prose words: bare parenthetical on the sentence stating the pattern, summarizing clause in the references entry.

Cast once on the hypothesis-graph paper, 2026-08-07: four queries, six hits, five dropped, one changed the framing of the central claim (Kamoi et al. 2024, whose survey predicts the ablation's plateau). Cost, one parenthetical.
