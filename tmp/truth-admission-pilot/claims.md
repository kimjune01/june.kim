# Frozen claim docket

Pilot frozen before reading the resolution filings. Source filings are the companies' 10-Ks filed in 2023; resolution filings are their next 10-Ks.

## Admission rule

A claim is admitted only if the source text itself specifies (1) an observable, (2) a direction or numeric threshold, (3) a deadline, and (4) a later public source capable of resolving it. We do not invent meanings for adjectives such as “moderate,” “improve,” or “approximately.”

## UPS — 2022 10-K, filed 2023-02-21

1. **Domestic average daily volume growth declines in H1 2023, then grows through the rest of 2023.** Falsified if the reported year-over-year growth sequence does not decline in H1 and subsequently rise. Resolution: 2023 quarterly disclosures summarized in the next 10-K. **Admitted, but compound.**
2. **Domestic revenue per piece grows moderately in 2023.** “Moderately” has no threshold. **Not admitted.**
3. **Domestic fuel surcharge revenue falls in 2023.** Falsified if 2023 domestic fuel surcharge revenue is not below 2022. Resolution: next 10-K. **Admitted.**
4. **Domestic cost-per-piece growth is elevated in Q1 2023 and moderates thereafter.** “Elevated” and “moderates” lack thresholds, though a monotone interpretation is possible. **Not admitted.**
5. **International year-over-year volume growth is negative in H1 2023 and improves in H2.** Falsified if either condition fails. Resolution: quarterly disclosures summarized in next 10-K. **Admitted, but compound.**
6. **International revenue per piece is relatively flat in 2023.** “Relatively flat” has no threshold. **Not admitted.**

## FedEx — FY2023 10-K, filed 2023-07-17

7. **FY2024 operating income improves because of DRIVE.** The outcome has direction and deadline, but the causal attribution has no isolating test. **Outcome admitted; causal explanation not admitted.**
8. **FY2024 capital expenditures are approximately $5.7 billion.** No tolerance is supplied for “approximately.” **Not admitted as written; record absolute forecast error descriptively.**
9. **FY2024 business-optimization costs are approximately $500 million.** No tolerance is supplied. **Not admitted as written; record absolute forecast error descriptively.**
10. **Annualized savings from the European business realignment are approximately $275 million beginning in FY2024.** No tolerance is supplied and the later filing must separately disclose realized savings. **Not admitted as written.**
11. **The One FedEx organizational redesign is fully implemented in June 2024.** Falsified if the next 10-K reports delay or incomplete implementation. Resolution: next 10-K. **Admitted.**
12. **FedEx begins reporting a resegmented structure in Q1 FY2025 after completing consolidation.** Falsified if the next 10-K does not announce the resegmentation for Q1 FY2025. **Admitted.**

Frozen counts: 12 claims; 5 fully admitted, 1 outcome-only, 6 not admitted. The pilot's first readout is resolution integrity, not returns.
